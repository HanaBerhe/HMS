1. Existing HMS Inspection & Reuse Architecture

To avoid code duplication and unnecessary architectural complexity, the ICU module integrates directly into existing core HMS databases:

  • Master Patient Index (MPI): Demographics, MRN, Code Status, and verified Drug Allergies stream directly into the sticky patient header.
  • Bed Management: Reuses existing hospital bed tables, adding critical-care equipment attributes (Ventilator Capable, Dialysis Ready, Isolation) and a 5-stage bed hygiene state engine.
  • Observation Repository: Reuses the timestamped hospital clinical observation store, presenting an hourly time-series flowsheet matrix.
  • Medication Administration (eMAR): Preserves a modular container hook for the Inpatient Department (IPD) eMAR schema once provided.
Anti-Over-Engineering Policy: Routine nursing entries (hourly vitals, feeds, and drain outputs) commit directly to the active ledger with zero intermediate managerial approval gates. Dual-witness signatures are strictly limited to high-alert medications and shift handovers.

3. Screen UI-02: Comprehensive Bedside Flowsheet (Hourly Matrix)

Purpose: Bedside nurse interface for charting hourly time-series vital signs, ventilator mechanics, and neurological scores.

  • Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP): Calculated automatically on cell blur upon entry of Systolic (S) and Diastolic (D):
    MAP = round((Systolic + 2 * Diastolic) / 3)
  • Non-Blocking Alert Prompts: Values outside typical limits trigger a soft inline confirmation prompt rather than hard-stopping the user from submitting a genuine clinical observation.

4. Screen UI-03: Specialized Nutrition & Fluid Monitoring

Purpose: Implements the exact confirmed fields for enteral feeding, oral diet, and bodily outputs, with a real-time net fluid balance ledger.

Category Preserved Confirmed Fields Validation & Calculation Rules
NG Nutrition DateTime, Type, Amount (ml), Frequency, Comments, Nurse Amount must be > 0 ml. Frequency: Continuous (ml/hr) vs. Bolus. Nurse auto-filled.
Oral Nutrition DateTime, Type, Amount (ml), Frequency, Comments, Nurse Amount must be > 0 ml. Diet consistency dropdown: Sips, Clear Liquids, Pureed, Oral Supplement.
Fluid Loss DateTime, Fluid Loss Source, Amount (ml), Comments, Nurse Standard dropdown (Urine, Drain, Chest Tube, Stool, Emesis, Other). Unmeasured checkbox sets volume to NULL without forcing zero.
Fluid Balance DateTime, Total Intake, Total Loss, Total Balance, Comment, Nurse Total Balance = Total Intake - Total Loss
If unmeasured entries exist, displays explicit warning: *Estimated / Unmeasured Loss Present.
Non-Destructive Correction Cascade: When an entry is corrected, the database flags the original entry as Superseded and logs the revised value, author, timestamp, and mandatory Reason for Correction. The engine automatically recalculates all subsequent cumulative balances without manual re-entry.

5. Screen UI-04: Structured Nursing Shift Handover

Purpose: Standardizes shift transitions using the SBAR framework (Situation, Background, Assessment, Recommendation), pairing auto-compiled chart telemetry with dual electronic authentication.

  • System Pre-Aggregation: Vitals ranges, 24h intake/loss totals, active line inventory, and open care tasks compile automatically onto the handoff sheet.
  • Clinical Narrative Entry: Outgoing nurse documents the clinical narrative, targets for the next shift, and urgent concerns.
  • Dual Electronic Sign-Off: Outgoing and incoming nurses enter credentials to finalize handoff. The record freezes into an immutable snapshot, and active tasks transfer to the incoming nurse.

6. Screen UI-05: Patient Step-Down Transfer & Bed Reset

Purpose: Coordinates transfer orders, line removal checklists, receiving ward acceptance, and initiates bed decontamination.

  • Physician Disposition Order: Destination ward, confirmed bed number, and discharge summary.
  • Nursing Transfer Checklist: Line disposition (Arterial line, CVC, Foley catheter status) and transport oxygen readiness.
  • Physical Dispatch: Nurse clicks Dispatch Patient → closes ICU encounter, resets bed to Cleaning Required, and alerts environmental services.
